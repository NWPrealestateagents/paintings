About

The painter

聂泽昭 is my grandfather. He paints in the traditional Chinese style with brush, ink and color on paper: birds and flowers, animals, and mountain landscapes.

Every painting here is an original, painted by his hand, so no two are the same.

The shop

I'm Wyatt, his grandson. I started selling his paintings at local markets this year, and this site is how the people we meet there can keep up with his new work.

My grandparents photograph each new batch. I put the paintings online, handle the orders, and pack and ship them myself.
